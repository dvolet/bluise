03 DEVELOPMENT PHILOSOPHY

DON'T JUST WRITE CODE. SOLVE PROBLEMS.

Every meaningful application begins with a problem worth solving.

The development process begins by understanding the purpose behind the product, the people who will use it and the outcome it is expected to achieve.

From there, ideas are translated into interfaces, architecture, data systems and functional software.

Development does not end when the application works. Testing, refinement, maintenance and continuous learning remain part of the process.
